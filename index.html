CONTROLS

Reefs only show on the radar when the sweep or a sonar ping passes over them, and they fade again. A vessel with an amber ring is about to hit a reef you can see. A red ring means two vessels are on a collision course. Red blips are unknown contacts that hunt ships. Ping them away.
